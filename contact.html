Jurisdiction

Assembly Constituency Mandals

Bhupalpalle constituency (No. 108) covers extensive agrarian and mining habitations across Jayashankar Bhupalpally district.

Constituency Center

Bhupalpalle Mandal

District administrative headquarters, Singareni Collieries Company Ltd (SCCL) coal mining units, Kakatiya Thermal Power Station (KTPS) nearby.

Native Mandal

Ghanpur (Mulug)

Historic agrarian heartland featuring Ghanapur irrigation reservoir, Chalivagu canal networks, and Buddharam native village.

Cultural Center

Regonda Mandal

Site of Sri Lakshmi Narasimha Swamy Temple at Kotanchu, Kasturba Gandhi Balika Vidyalaya (KGBV), and 33/11 KV electrical feeder sub-stations.

Transit Hub

Chityal Mandal

Regional highway junction, newly sanctioned modern bus station, and connecting link between agricultural mandals and district commercial centers.

Agrarian Belt

Shayampet Mandal

Predominantly paddy, chilli, and cotton farming community benefiting from Devadula canal extensions and welfare distribution.

Agrarian Belt

Mogullapalle Mandal

Extensive revenue villages with ongoing rural Anganwadi buildings, Gram Panchayat secretariats, and rural feeder road upgrades.
