Public Work & Legislative Action

A comprehensive record of developmental projects, departmental reviews, infrastructure sanctions, and state assembly interventions documented across reputable regional media.

Note on Verification & Attribution

The initiatives below are documented from public media reports, administrative proceedings, and legislative records. Language reflects verified representative roles (such as announced sanction, laid foundation stone, participated in inauguration, directed officials, and raised in legislative assembly) and should be read as public representation rather than sole individual execution.

Irrigation Network ₹31 Crore Sanction

Ghanapur Reservoir Canals Modernization

Announced sanction of ₹31 crore for the modernization and concrete lining of four major crop canals under the Ghanapur reservoir. Works stabilize water supply for 44,000 acres utilizing Devadula lift irrigation networks and Sriram Sagar Project (SRSP) allocations.

Status: Tenders initiated · 12-month completion schedule · Reported by The Hans India & Dailyhunt
Heritage & Pilgrim Amenities ₹12.15 Crore Sanction

Kotanchu Sri Lakshmi Narasimha Swamy Temple Upgrade

Special development initiative securing ₹12.15 crore for the comprehensive heritage conservation, pilgrim queue complexes, dining halls, and tourism infrastructure at Sri Lakshmi Narasimha Swamy Temple in Kotanchu village, Regonda mandal.

Status: Construction progressing · Reported by The Hans India & V6 Velugu

Legislative Record

Assembly Interventions & Questions

Documented matters raised by Gandra Satyanarayana Rao during session debates in the 3rd Telangana Legislative Assembly.

September 18, 2026 · Telangana Legislative Assembly Singareni Welfare

Singareni Coal Miners & Displaced Persons Rights

Highlighted the rights of Singareni Collieries coal miners, demanded pension enhancements for retired employees, urged fair conditions and safety benefits for contract workforce, and pressed for comprehensive rehabilitation for families displaced by open-cast mining operations.

Reported by Andhra Prabha & HMTV Legislative Assembly coverage
September 16, 2026 · Telangana Legislative Assembly Agriculture & Storage

Crop Diversification & Cold Storage Facilities

Emphasized the urgent necessity of state-supported cold storage facilities in Bhupalpally to safeguard perishable vegetable harvests. Raised issues regarding monkey menace affecting vegetable cultivation and requested timely supply of oil palm saplings to support farmer incomes.

Reported by Telangana Today & The Hindu Assembly Reports
March 26, 2026 · Budget Session Irrigation & Security

Constituency Water Distribution & Women's Police Stations

Addressed irrigation water distribution challenges across tail-end mandals, highway traffic congestion at urban intersections, and formally requested the establishment of dedicated Women's Police Stations in the constituency.

Reported by Andhra Prabha
August 07, 2026 · Chalivagu Reservoir Site Water Rights

Protection of Chalivagu Project Water Levels

Inspected water gauges when irrigation levels dropped below 17 feet. Warned authorities against unauthorized diversion and committed to peaceful public agitation alongside local sarpanches and farmers to secure local agrarian rights.

Reported by V6 Velugu
