About the Person

A lifetime dedicated to agriculture, grassroots democracy, and direct constituent representation.

Born in 1965 to agrarian elder Late Sri Gandra Samma Rao in Buddharam village, Ghanapuram mandal, Gandra Satyanarayana Rao (widely known as "Sathenna") completed his Bachelor of Arts from Kakatiya University and an ITI Civil diploma. Over nearly four decades of dedicated public service—starting as Village Sarpanch in Buddharam, then ZPTC Member, and ultimately winning the Bhupalpalle Assembly seat (108) in December 2023 with 123,116 votes and a historic 52,699-vote victory margin—he has stood as an unwavering champion for farmers' canal waters, Singareni coal miners, and rural infrastructure.

The Constituency

Bhupalpalle Assembly Constituency (108)

Jayashankar Bhupalpally district is characterized by agricultural agrarian landscapes, Singareni Collieries coal deposits, and thermal power generation.

Encompassing key mandals including Bhupalpalle, Ghanpur (Mulug), Regonda, Shayampet, Chityal, and Mogullapalle, the area demands sustained policy focus on canal water tail-end delivery, rural road connectivity, tribal hamlets welfare, and mining worker safety.
